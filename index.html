Who We Are

Security Mate is an Australian, locally owned and operated security provider. Our leadership team brings real-world security experience to every site, ensuring all officers deployed in high-visibility protective wear are fully trained, briefed, and held to strict standards.

Because we've worked on active construction zones, distribution hubs, and commercial properties ourselves, we understand the critical necessity of site access control, asset protection, and safety compliance.

We focus specifically on high-impact, unarmed static guarding and site patrols—done consistently and safely on every shift.
