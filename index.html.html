Who we are

Security Mate is an Australian, locally owned and operated security company. Our owner has worked in the security industry themselves — not just managed it on paper — and that hands-on experience shapes how every site is assessed, every guard is briefed, and every shift is held to account.

Because we've stood the posts ourselves, we know the difference between a guard who's just present and one who's actually paying attention. That's the standard we hold every engagement to.

We keep our service narrow on purpose: unarmed static guarding, done consistently, rather than a long list of services spread thin.

What we do

Static, unarmed guarding and mobile patrols for sites that need a visible, dependable presence.

Site briefing

Every guard is briefed on your procedures, access points and escalation contacts before the first shift.

Mobile patrols

Scheduled or randomised patrol runs across multi-site or perimeter locations, with checkpoint logging.

24/7 coverage

Day, night, weekend and public holiday shifts, covered at rates aligned to the Security Services Award.

Incident logging

Access and incident records kept for every shift, so you have a clear paper trail if anything happens.

Fast response

Call, text or email and reach us directly to scope urgent or short-notice coverage.
